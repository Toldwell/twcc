Tiles: Camera Control

By Toldwell. Control cinema cameras from iPhone, iPad and Mac, over the network or a cable: Sony, Blackmagic Design and ARRI cameras, side by side, on pages of tiles you lay out yourself. Stream Deck and Bitfocus Companion can drive it too.

Support

Email william@toldwell.com. Say which camera and which iPhone, iPad or Mac, and paste Tiles' activity log: Settings → Copy Activity Log. The version is in Settings → About Tiles.

Support for Tiles comes from Toldwell alone. Sony, Blackmagic Design and ARRI don't make, own, license or support it.

Stream Deck and Companion

Tiles' Control API drives it from a Stream Deck or Bitfocus Companion: the plugin and the module.
