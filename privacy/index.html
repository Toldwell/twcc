Tiles: Camera Control privacy policy

Tiles: Camera Control (Tiles, made by Toldwell) doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

The app talks only to devices on your local network: the cameras you add, a Stream Deck or Companion controller if you set one up, and, once you set up HaLow, the HaLow radio nodes there, which it finds itself (it asks devices that advertise a web page whether they're one). Camera passwords, the HaLow nodes' password and the Control API token are kept in your device's Keychain. Your camera list and layouts stay on your device. Nothing is sent to Toldwell or to anyone else.
